# GoServe Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

GoServe provides a messaging-based service that helps customers communicate with businesses and request services through channels such as WhatsApp.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you interact with a business through GoServe.

## Information We Process

When you communicate with a business using GoServe, we may process information such as:

- your phone number or messaging account identifier;
- messages and information you send through the service;
- information needed to identify your relationship with the business you are contacting;
- service, delivery, booking, site, or other relevant business context;
- requests, selections, confirmations, and other actions you make through the service; and
- technical information required to operate and secure the service.

Please do not send sensitive information unless it is necessary for the service you are requesting.

## How We Use Information

Information processed through GoServe may be used to:

- identify the appropriate customer or business relationship;
- understand and process customer requests;
- retrieve relevant information from the business providing the service;
- submit, update, or manage authorised service requests;
- provide confirmations and status information;
- transfer a request to an appropriate person when human assistance is required;
- maintain security and reliability; and
- investigate technical or service issues.

GoServe does not treat AI-generated interpretations as authoritative business information. Where GoServe connects to a business system, authoritative information such as availability, pricing, service status, and accepted transactions comes from the relevant business or its systems.

## Businesses Using GoServe

GoServe may process information on behalf of businesses that use the service.

The business you are communicating with may have its own privacy policy and may independently collect, store, or use information relating to its customers and services.

## Service Providers

GoServe may use third-party service providers to operate the service.

For example, when WhatsApp is used as the communication channel, messaging information is transmitted through services provided by Meta and WhatsApp and is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Other infrastructure or business-system providers may process information where necessary to provide the requested service.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with the business you are communicating with and with service providers where necessary to operate GoServe, process your request, provide the requested service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of the service.

## Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing the service, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving issues, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention requirements may also depend on the business you are communicating with and the systems that business uses.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information processed through GoServe.

No internet or messaging service can guarantee absolute security.

## Access, Correction and Deletion

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your use of GoServe, request correction of information, or request deletion where applicable.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, security, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes.

Separate data deletion instructions are available through GoServe's Data Deletion page.

## New Zealand Privacy

GoServe is being developed in New Zealand. Where applicable, personal information is handled in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and other applicable privacy requirements.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as GoServe develops or as its services and legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published at this location.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to GoServe, please contact the operator of GoServe through the contact details provided by the business through which you accessed the service.
