# GoServe Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

GoServe users may request deletion of personal information associated with their use of the service.

## How to Request Deletion

To request deletion, contact the business through which you use GoServe and state that you would like your GoServe-related personal information deleted.

Please provide enough information for the request to be identified, such as the phone number or messaging account used to communicate with the business.

## What Happens Next

The request will be reviewed and applicable personal information associated with your use of GoServe will be identified and deleted where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business record-keeping requirements.

Information held independently by the business you communicated with, Meta, WhatsApp, or another third-party service provider may be subject to that organisation's own deletion processes and retention requirements.

## Questions

For questions about deletion of information processed through GoServe, contact the business through which you accessed GoServe.
